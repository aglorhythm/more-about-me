# 🌸 My EntreprenHER Journey 🌸

**My EntreprenHER Journey** is a personal repository documenting how entrepreneurship led me to explore business, technology, operations, and information systems.

What started with ideas around fashion, e-commerce, branding, and digital products gradually became a much broader journey into building platforms, managing operations, connecting tools, automating workflows, analysing business needs, and understanding the systems behind a business.

This repository is both an archive of that journey and a portfolio of the projects, experiments, skills, and lessons that shaped the way I work today.

It reflects a path that has taken me from **Communication & Marketing → Entrepreneurship → Business Analysis → DevOps & Cloud → Enterprise Architecture & Operational Excellence**.


### 🌱 What you'll discover inside

- **Entrepreneurial projects** — products, platforms, e-commerce concepts, and businesses I've built or explored
- **Business & operations** — how I approached processes, customer journeys, tools, workflows, and day-to-day operations
- **Technology** — web development, APIs, cloud, automation, data, and the technical systems behind my projects
- **Skills** — the multidisciplinary skill set I've developed across business and technology
- **Experiments** — ideas, prototypes, technical explorations, and things I've tested along the way
- **Reflections** — lessons from entrepreneurship, including what worked, what didn't, and how my thinking evolved


## 🧩 From entrepreneurship to systems thinking

Entrepreneurship taught me that building a business isn't just about having a good product or a strong brand.

Behind every business is a system:

**people → processes → data → applications → technology**

Building my own projects made me increasingly interested in how those pieces interact — and what happens when they don't.

That curiosity eventually led me into **Business Analysis (AMOA)**, **DevOps & Cloud**, automation, and now **Enterprise Architecture**.

Today, I'm particularly interested in the connection between:

**Business Strategy • Information Systems • Enterprise Architecture • Operational Excellence • Cloud • Automation**

I'm also the founder of [PrettySecure](https://prettysecure.cloud), where this combination of business, operations, and technology continues to shape the way I think about organizations and their systems.

My path hasn't been linear, and that's exactly what made it valuable.

I started by teaching myself how to build and market digital projects. Entrepreneurship pushed me into technology. Technology led me deeper into information systems. Professional experiences in Business Analysis and DevOps gave me another perspective on how larger organizations operate.
